Questions photographers ask

Does it compete with my photos?

No. Guest photos are the casual phone shots from tables and the dance floor. Your work stays the centrepiece, and the film is built from your photos.

Yes: the guest page, the QR card, the handover and the keepsake. A small "Powered by GatherWall" line stays at the bottom.

How long is everything kept online?

90 days after the event, and the couple can download every photo and message in one tap to keep.

Is there a monthly fee?
