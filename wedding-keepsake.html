Questions

The things couples ask

How long does it take?
About two weeks from the day you send your photos and clips. If you need it for a specific date, tell us first and we'll confirm before you pay.
What do you need from us?
Your photos and any video clips, plus the story behind the day. A short guided form walks you through it — you don't need to write anything polished.
Can the songs be in our language?
Yes. We write natively in English and Czech, and can mix both in one song. Other languages on request — ask before you buy.
How are the songs actually made?
The lyrics are written by hand from your story. The music is produced through our AI studio pipeline and reviewed before delivery, so each arrangement is original to you rather than a stock track. We're upfront about this because we think the words are what matter.
Who can see our page?
Only the people you send the link to. It's private by default and never listed publicly or indexed by search engines.
We've already had our wedding. Is it too late?
Not at all. Most of what we build is made after the day, from photos you already have. Anniversaries are one of the most common reasons people order.
