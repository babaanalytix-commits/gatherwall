For the party

Put every guest’s love on the big screen at your owambe

Guests scan, post a message or photo, and it appears live. No app, no sign-up.

At a big Nigerian party, hundreds of people want to say something to the celebrant and only a handful get the microphone. A live tribute wall gives everyone a voice.

How it works on the night

  1. Put the QR code on the tables, the programme or the screen.
  2. Guests scan it with their phone camera and type a message, add a photo, or both.
  3. Messages float onto the big screen in real time, in your colours.
  4. Family abroad can post too, from anywhere, using the same link.

Why hosts love it

  • It fills the gaps between the entrance, the food and the dancing.
  • Everyone is included: the quiet aunty, the friends in Toronto, the colleagues who couldn't come.
  • You keep it. Every message and photo stays in one place for the celebrant afterwards.
  • You stay in control. Turn on approval so nothing appears until you've seen it.
Works for weddings, birthdays, thanksgivings and send-offs.

Ask your event planner or MC to open the wall on the venue screen. It works on any laptop, TV or projector with internet.

Questions

Do guests need to download an app?

No. They scan the code with their phone camera and post from the browser.

Can we stop inappropriate messages?

Yes. Turn on approval and only messages you approve appear on screen.

What do we need at the venue?

A screen or projector, a laptop or smart TV, and an internet connection. Mobile data hotspots work too.

Can family abroad post?

Yes. Share the same link in WhatsApp groups and their messages appear alongside everyone else’s.
