Weddings

Your love story, sung, shared and kept

An original song about how you met, a guest message wall for the reception, and a film to keep.

From the introduction to the traditional engagement to the white wedding, a Nigerian wedding is many celebrations. Here are keepsakes that tie them together.

Ideas couples love

A song about how you met

Where you met, who made the first move, what your families said, the inside jokes. Played for the couple's entrance or the first dance, or given as a surprise by the bride's or groom's family.

A live wall at the reception

Guests scan a code and post wishes and photos that appear on the big screen. Relatives abroad can post too. You keep every message.

A film of your story

Childhood photos of you both, the courtship, the families, set to your song. Perfect for the reception screen or the anniversary.

A gift from family abroad

Can't make it to the wedding in Nigeria? Commission the couple's song and send it to be played at the reception. It's a gift that arrives on time and in full.

Questions

Can the song mix English with Yoruba, Igbo or another language?

Yes. Tell us the phrases, meanings and pronunciation and we weave them in.

How early should we book a wedding film?

At least three to four weeks before the wedding. Start a free plan to see your exact dates.

Can guests post at both the traditional and white wedding?

Yes. Use one wall for both, or a separate wall for each.

Can family abroad pay for it as a gift?

Yes. Anyone can pay by card from anywhere.
