Questions

Is the whole song in Yoruba?

Most songs are in English with Yoruba phrases woven in, which suits mixed-language families. Tell us how much Yoruba you want.

Can you include oriki?

Yes, if you give us the lines. We only use what the family provides, so it stays accurate and respectful.

Will the Yoruba be pronounced correctly?

Write how each word sounds and we guide the vocal with it. Listen to both versions and choose the one that sounds right.

Can I do Igbo or Hausa instead?

Yes, the same way: tell us the phrases, meanings and pronunciation.
