05 · On the day

Making the moment go smoothly

  • Play the film from a USB stick or laptop, not the internet. Church Wi-Fi is never reliable.
  • Test it on the real projector and sound system the week before (around 1 December).
  • Keep the phone version on a second device as a backup.
  • Ask someone to film the room while it plays: Sam's face, the family singing.
