Milestone birthdays

Ideas for a 70th they’ll talk about for years

Beyond the aso-ebi, the cake and the speeches: something that tells their story.

A 70th in a Nigerian family is a thanksgiving, a reunion and a party in one. The aso-ebi is chosen, the hall is booked, the jollof is sorted. What's often missing is a moment that's truly about the celebrant's life.

Seven ideas that put their story at the centre

  1. A song written from their life. Their hometown, their work, how they raised the family, the sayings everyone repeats. Played during the cake cutting or before the speeches, it stops the room.
  2. A tribute wall from everyone who can't attend. Relatives abroad, old colleagues, school friends add messages and photos to one link. Show it on the screen while guests arrive.
  3. A "this is your life" photo film. Old black-and-white photos, the wedding, the children, the grandchildren, set to their song.
  4. Seventy messages. Ask seventy people for one line each. A wall makes collecting them easy.
  5. Honour those who've passed. A gentle mention of parents or a spouse who can't be there. Tell us how you'd like them remembered.
  6. A family choir moment. Share the lyrics so children and grandchildren can sing along to the chorus.
  7. A keepsake afterwards. A private page with the song, film and messages that the celebrant can replay any time.

A simple timeline

  • 4 weeks before: start the story and collect photos from siblings.
  • 3 weeks before: open the tribute wall to the family WhatsApp groups.
  • 1 week before: approve the song and film; send the file to whoever runs the screen.
  • On the day: play the film after the thanksgiving or before the cake.
