How to make it land on the day

  1. Tell one person at home. A sibling, cousin or neighbour who'll press play on the TV or speaker after church.
  2. Send the file the day before. Download the song or film and send it on WhatsApp, so it plays even if the network is slow.
  3. Use the names only family uses. Mummy, Baba, Iya, Nne, the nickname. That's what makes the room go quiet.
  4. Call straight after. Ring them on video the moment it finishes.

Questions

What is the latest I can order?

The Christmas song is ready within minutes, so you can order it right up to Christmas Eve. Open a family Christmas wall about two weeks before so everyone has time to add to it. The made-for-you film needs about three weeks, so order it by 4 December.

Will it work for my parents in Nigeria on a slow network?

Yes. Everything opens from one link on a phone, with no app and no account. You can also download the song or film and send the file on WhatsApp so it plays offline.

Can the song include Yoruba, Igbo or Pidgin?

Yes. Tell us the phrases and what they mean, and we write them in. You read the full lyrics free before you pay, and can change anything.

Can my brothers and sisters abroad join in?

Yes. Share one link in the family WhatsApp group. Everyone adds messages and photos to the wall, or sends a short video for the film.

How do I pay from the UK, US or Canada?

By card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, in US dollars. The price you see is the price you pay. Your parents never pay or sign up for anything.
