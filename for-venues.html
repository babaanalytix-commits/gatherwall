How it works for your team

  1. Your venue gets its own branded page with your offers, where customers can ask about an event.
  2. Create each event in your dashboard in 20 seconds. QR table cards are ready to print.
  3. On the day the wall runs on your screens. Staff moderate from a phone.
  4. Afterwards, hand it over to your customer in one click. They keep everything, and remember where it happened.

How you make money

You set the price. Include it in your packages or sell it as an upgrade. We charge a small platform fee plus a charge per activated event, so you keep a clear margin. Founding partners get their first event free, and we agree pricing together after the pilot.
