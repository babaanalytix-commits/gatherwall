Made-for-you service: stages, changes, deadlines & refunds

  • Before you book. You create a free project, see your plan and timeline, and give us the story. You book only when the essentials are complete, so work can start straight away.
  • Your project page. Every draft, approval and change request happens on your private project page. Each one is time-stamped and forms the record of what was agreed. WhatsApp and email are for questions.
  • Stages. (1) Lyrics, (2) Song, (3) Photos, (4) Film. Each stage is approved before the next begins. Once a stage is approved it is locked: the song is recorded from the approved lyrics, and the film is built from the approved song and the confirmed photo set.
  • Rounds of changes. Story Film includes 1 round on the lyrics and 1 on the film; Signature includes 2 of each. The song stage includes a choice of versions and one style redo. A round is one message containing all your changes, sent within the review window.
  • Review window. Please reply to each draft within 5 days. We remind you before it ends. If we don't hear from you within 5 days, that stage is treated as approved and we continue, so your date is protected.
  • After approval. Changes to an approved stage are not included. If something must change, ask us: where it's possible we'll quote first (extra rounds are $29), and nothing is done or charged without your agreement.
  • Changes vs new work. Fixes such as a word, a name, swapping or reordering photos are changes. A new concept, a new song or a new film is a new order.
  • Material deadlines. Photos confirmed at least 21 days before the event (Signature) or 14 days (Story Film). Tribute walls close 14 days before the event. Photos added after you confirm the set are not included. If materials arrive late, the delivery date may move or rush pricing (+$50) may apply.
