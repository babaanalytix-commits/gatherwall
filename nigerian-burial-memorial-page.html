In loving memory

A place for the whole family to remember, wherever they are

Their life story, photos, candles and memories, shared in one link before and after the burial.

When someone we love passes, the family is often spread across Lagos, London, Houston and beyond. Not everyone can travel for the burial. A memorial page lets everyone take part in the remembering.

What a memorial page holds

  • Their life story: the eulogy or a short biography, with their photo and dates.
  • Memories from everyone: family and friends add their own stories, wherever they live.
  • Candles: a simple way for people to show they're thinking of you.
  • One link to share with the burial programme, in WhatsApp groups or on social media.

Before, during and after the burial

  1. Before: share the link with the announcement so people abroad can send condolences and memories.
  2. On the day: read some of the memories aloud, or show the page during the reception.
  3. After: keep it for remembrance anniversaries. Many families return every year.
Remembrance anniversaries

For a one-year or three-year remembrance, some families add a song written from the person's life. Our founder made one for his own father's three-year remembrance: hear “A song for Dad”.

Anyone with the link. You can share it widely or only with family.

Can I edit the page later?

Yes. You get a private edit link; keep it safe, because only that link can change the page.

Yes. Many families use it for one-year and three-year remembrances.
