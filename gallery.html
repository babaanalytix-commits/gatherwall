Invite your photographer

Song of the Night
The song every guest’s film is set to. Paste a gatherwall.com or media.gatherwall.com link and the second it should start from (usually the chorus).
Highlights
Tap a photo, then ⭐ to make it a highlight. Every guest’s film starts with their own photos plus your highlights (aim for about 15). You can also hide photos.
