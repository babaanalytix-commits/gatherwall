Questions

How is this different from the $39 Keepsake Song?

The $39 song is instant: you answer a few questions and the song is created for you automatically. The made-for-you service is personal — we talk with you, gather the family's photos, refine the lyrics and pronunciation with you, and build the film by hand.

How long does it take?

About 2–3 weeks from receiving your story and photos. Tell us your date on the free call and we'll confirm we can make it.

Can the song include Yoruba, Igbo, Twi or another language?

Yes. We spell each phrase the way it is spoken so it sounds right, and you hear it before anything is final.

What if we don't like it?

You review every draft before anything is final. If the finished piece doesn't move you, we rewrite it once free — and if you're still not happy, we refund you in full.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything is on private links that only your family has. Nothing is shared publicly without your permission.
