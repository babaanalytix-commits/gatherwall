Questions

How is this different from the $39 Keepsake Song?

The $39 song is instant: you answer a few questions and the song is created for you automatically. The made-for-you service is personal — we talk with you, gather the family's photos, refine the lyrics and pronunciation with you, and build the film by hand.

How long does it take?

About 2–3 weeks from receiving your story and photos. Please book at least 3 weeks ahead for a Story Film and 4 weeks ahead for a Signature Celebration. Message us your date first if it's sooner and we'll tell you honestly if we can make it.

Can the song include Yoruba, Igbo, Twi or another language?

Yes. We spell each phrase the way it is spoken so it sounds right, and you hear it before anything is final.

What if we don't like it?

You review every draft before anything is final. If the first lyrics aren't right, we rewrite them once free; if you're still not happy at that point, we refund 50%. Once the film draft has been shared we don't refund, but we keep refining within your included rounds of changes. If we miss your delivery date (with your story and photos received on time), you get a full refund.

How many changes can we ask for?

Story Film includes 1 round of changes on the lyrics and 1 on the film; Signature Celebration includes 2 of each. A round is one message with all your changes, sent within 5 days of receiving the draft. Once you approve a stage it is locked, so the next stage is built on firm ground. If we don't hear back within 5 days, that stage counts as approved and we carry on, so your date is never at risk. Extra rounds are $29 each. Small fixes (a word, a name, swapping photos) are changes; a brand-new concept or song is a new order.

When do we pay, and what do you need from us?

Nothing to pay to start: you create your project, see your plan and timeline, and tell us the story. You pay in full when you book, once the essentials are complete; that reserves your date and we start straight away. Please confirm your photos at least 21 days before the event (Signature) or 14 days before (Story Film); the tribute wall closes 14 days before the event so the book can be made. If anything arrives late, the delivery date may move.

Whose photos and music can we use?

Please only send photos you're happy for us to use, and make sure the people featured are happy to be included. Every song is original and written for you, so you can play it anywhere. We may decline content that's offensive or unlawful.

Is it private?

Yes. Everything is on private links that only your family has. Nothing is shared publicly without your permission.
