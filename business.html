Questions pros ask

Will it make more work for me on the night?

No. You create the event in advance, the QR goes on the tables or your screen, and guests do the rest. You or the host can approve messages from a phone.

Is my logo on everything?

Yes: the guest page, the big screen, the QR card, the handover and the keepsake. A small "Powered by GatherWall" line stays at the bottom.

What if the venue Wi-Fi is weak?

Guests post over their own mobile data. The big screen only needs a basic connection, and it keeps showing what it has if the signal drops.

Can I charge whatever I like?

Yes. Your customers pay you. We charge $4 per event you run, plus any songs you add.

Do I need a website?

No. You get your own branded GatherWall page to share on Instagram or WhatsApp. If you do have a website, you can also embed the guest form or wall with two lines of code.

What happens if I stop using GatherWall?

Nothing breaks for your couples: events you've run stay online for their full 90 days, and anything they've downloaded is theirs. You simply stop paying. No contract, no monthly fee.

What does the couple keep?

Their own keepsake page with every message and photo, online for 90 days and downloadable in one tap, so it's theirs to keep. Your name is on it.
